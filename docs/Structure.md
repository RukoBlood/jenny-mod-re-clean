# Project Structure

- blocks (Contains the fake fire block used by kobolds.)
- command (In-game console commands)
- companion (Contains the core companion behavior and shared AI goals.)
  - *.java (Shared companion AI goals and general-purpose companion behavior.)
  - fighter (Contains combat-oriented companion AI logic.)
  - supporter (Contains support-oriented companion AI logic, only used by Bee.)
- deprecated (Contains deprecated or removed content that remains in the source.)
  - NYIWinchesterItem (Contains the unfinished Winchester item. The code remains in the project, but the item is no longer functional.)
- events (Contains Mod event handlers.)
- gender_change (Contains the systems responsible for changing the player's model to predefined characters such as Jenny, Ellie, and others.)
  - hornypotion (Contains the horny potion and its related transformation mechanics.)
- girls (Contains the entities and character-specific logic for the mod's girls.)
  - Allie (Allie, the genie girl, and her character-specific logic.)
    - lamp (Contains Allie's lamp and its related functionality.)
  - base (Contains base classes and shared functionality used by the girl entities.)
    - EditorWand (NPC editor wand used for editing and working with custom models.)
    - PlayerGirl (Contains the logic for players whose models have been changed using the horny potion system.)
  - Bee (Bee girl and her supporter-related behavior.)
  - Bia (Bia, the bear girl, and her entity-specific behavior.)
  - Custom (Contains custom model loading, management, and rendering logic.)
  - Ellie (Ellie girl and her entity-specific behavior.)
  - Galath (Galath, the succubus boss, including her boss and taming-related mechanics.)
    - EnergyBall (Contains the energy ball projectile used by Galath.)
    - GalathCoin (Contains the coin used to spawn Galath after defeating her.)
  - Goblin (Goblin girls, including their taming, companion, and combat behavior.)
  - Jenny (Jenny girl and her character-specific logic.)
  - Kobold (Tameable kobold girls and their resource-gathering behavior.)
    - DragonStaff (Tool used to control and interact with the kobolds.)
    - KoboldEgg (Kobold egg and its hatching mechanics.)
    - TribeEgg (Item used to spawn a kobold tribe.)
  - Luna (Luna, the catgirl, including her fishing-related behavior.)
    - LunaRod (Fishing rod specifically used by Luna.)
  - Manglelie (Manglelie, Galath's daughter. She exists as an entity but cannot be used as a player model.)
  - Pyrocynical (A rare hostile nextbot based on a british youtuber. It can spawn in the world and chase and attack players, but cannot be used as a player model.)
  - Slime (Slime girl and her associated mechanics.)
    - friendlySlime (Friendly slime that can spawn after breeding with the slime girl.)
- gui (Contains the graphical user interfaces used throughout the mod.)
  - CustomModel (Interface for managing and configuring custom models.)
  - Galath (Interfaces related to the Galath boss encounter, including the boss minigame and flight UI.)
  - GenderChange (Interface for changing the player's character model.)
  - Menu (Interface for interacting with the girls and accessing their available actions.)
  - sex (Interface for the sex system, including the black-screen transition.)
- Packets (Contains the network packets used for client-server communication.)
- proxy (Contains the common and client-side Forge proxy implementations.)
- util (General-purpose utility classes, including mathematical helpers, helper functions, and bidirectional maps.)
  - anim (Contains animation utilities, including the breast physics system.)
  - Handlers (Contains Forge registration and handler classes.)
  - interfaces (Contains interfaces used by mobs and other mod components.)
- world (Contains world generation logic and fake-world utilities used for testing and debugging.)
- Main.java (Main mod entry point and initialization class.)
